FIX6 addresses the AssistChecks failure:
  AssertionError: Explicit same-slot choice yields ownership

Root cause:
A short manual-override grace timer introduced by r8 correctly prevented immediate retake after a manual slot change, but the timer survived Auto Tool being disabled. Re-enabling Auto Tool immediately in the test therefore inherited stale ownership state.

Fix:
- Auto Tool disable clears toolYieldUntil after cleanup.
- Auto Soup disable clears soupYieldUntil, nextSoup and soupAge after cleanup.
- AssistChecks verifies the lifecycle reset before exercising explicit same-slot ownership.

No anti-cheat or evasion behavior is added.
